# Privacy Policy for My Photo Editor AI

**Last Updated:** 2026

## 1. Overview
**My Photo Editor AI** is an offline-first photo editing application built with strict user privacy in mind. Your photographs belong exclusively to you.

## 2. Zero Photo Collection & Offline Processing
- **No Cloud Uploads:** All image editing, filters, adjustments, and AI operations (background removal, portrait blur, face retouching, photo enhancement, restoration, and object removal) are performed **100% locally on your device**.
- **No Account Required:** We do not require registration, login, email addresses, or user profiles.
- **No Server Storage:** We do not operate backend servers to store, view, or analyze your images.

## 3. Permissions Used
- **Android Photo Picker:** Used only when you choose photos to import into the editor. The app does not scan or upload your gallery in the background.
- **Gallery Export (MediaStore):** Used only when you explicitly choose to save an edited photo to your device gallery.

## 4. Advertising (Optional Google AdMob)
- The app may display advertisements via **Google AdMob** when an internet connection is available.
- Core photo editing features work completely offline (including in Airplane Mode) without requiring internet access.
- **Your photos and editing data are never shared with or transmitted to advertising networks.**
- Google AdMob may collect non-photo device advertising identifiers in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and regional consent regulations (such as EEA/GDPR where applicable).

## 5. Local Storage
- Saved projects, drafts, favorite filters, and app preferences are stored locally on your device in an app-private SQLite database and local storage.
- You can clear cached files or delete projects anytime from within the app's **Projects** or **Settings** screens.

## 6. Contact
If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the app publisher via the contact email listed on the Google Play Store listing.
